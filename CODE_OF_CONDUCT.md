# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Zambezi NLU brings together clinicians, linguists, engineers and community members from different countries, languages and professions. We want everyone to be able to take part without harassment or disrespect, whatever their age, gender, ethnicity, nationality, language, religion, disability, health status, sexual orientation, level of experience or profession.

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone who takes part in the project: maintainers, reviewers, contributors and anyone who opens an issue or comments.

## Where it applies

- This repository: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and commit messages.
- Project email, calls, meetings and events.
- Any public place where someone is representing the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Explain what should change and why.
- Respect expertise of different kinds. A first-language speaker's judgement about wording and a clinician's judgement about safety both carry weight.
- Treat every language, dialect and way of speaking as valid. Do not mock spelling, grammar, accent or code-switching.
- Discuss sexual and reproductive health in a factual, professional way.
- Accept correction gracefully, and correct others kindly.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, threats or stalking.
- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about a person or group.
- Sexual language, imagery or advances directed at anyone. Clinical discussion of sexual and reproductive health is not a breach.
- Stigmatising language about people because of a health condition, pregnancy, HIV status, sexual history or reproductive choices.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing patient, user or other personal health information in any form, including in examples, screenshots and test data.
- Knowingly submitting false or unsafe health content, or content you do not have the right to share.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, or continuing behaviour after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct, email **developers@dawa-health.com**.

Please include:

- what happened, and where (a link if there is one);
- who was involved;
- when it happened;
- anything else that would help us understand it.

You do not need to have been the target of the behaviour to report it.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

## How reports are handled

- Reports are read by the project maintainers. We aim to acknowledge a report within five working days.
- The identity of the person reporting is kept confidential and is shared only with those who need it to handle the report.
- If a report concerns a maintainer or reviewer, that person takes no part in handling it. You can say in your email that you want the report to go to Dawa Health leadership instead of the maintainers.
- We will hear from the people involved before deciding what to do, unless that would put someone at risk.
- Nobody will be penalised for making a report in good faith.

## Consequences

Maintainers decide on a response in proportion to what happened. Possible responses are:

1. **A private note** explaining the problem and what is expected, for a minor or first issue.
2. **A formal warning**, with conditions such as staying out of a discussion for a period.
3. **A temporary ban** from taking part in the project.
4. **A permanent ban**, for serious or repeated breaches.

Maintainers may also remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct.

Sharing personal or patient information is treated as serious in every case. The content is removed as soon as it is found, including from the repository history where possible.

## Appeals

A person who disagrees with a decision can ask for it to be reviewed by emailing developers@dawa-health.com within 30 days. The review is done by someone at Dawa Health who was not involved in the original decision.

## Changes

Changes to this Code of Conduct follow the process in the [project charter](CHARTER.md).
